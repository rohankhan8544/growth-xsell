01 — Launch

The Amazon Launch Framework™

From product idea to first meaningful traction.

“How do I successfully enter Amazon?”

Primary objectiveEstablish traction

Plan my launch →See the framework

The three stages

  1. DiagnoseWeeks 1–2

    Is this niche winnable at your price? A go / no-go before you order inventory.

  2. BuildWeeks 3–6

    Listing, keyword map, launch campaigns, review plan and budget pacing — built to a 90-day plan.

  3. ActivateDays 1–90

    Launch week, the ranking push, daily ad management and a weekly scorecard.

If Diagnose says don’t launch, you stop there — and keep the inventory money. That outcome counts as a win.

Sound familiar?

You’re here because…

  • It’s your first product and you don’t know what a good launch looks like.
  • You’re not sure the niche can win — or what it costs to find out.
  • Nobody can tell you what ad budget is normal for the first 60 days.
  • Your listing was built on guesswork, not search data.
  • You launched once before. It didn’t land, and you’re not sure why.
  • You have an established brand and a new ASIN that can’t afford a slow start.

Why launches fail

Most launches are lost before launch day.

Wrong market, decided too late

Viability gets tested after the inventory is ordered — when the only option left is to push harder.

A listing written for the seller

Copy from the brand’s point of view, keywords bolted on afterwards. Shoppers search differently.

Ads switched on without structure

One auto campaign, no budget pacing, no ranking targets. Spend goes everywhere and ranks nothing.

No definition of traction

No target for reviews, rank or velocity — so nothing gets adjusted until the money’s gone.

The Launch Framework™

Three stages. One scorecard.

Every engagement runs the same three stages in the same order, and is reported on the same scorecard. That is what makes it a framework and not a service list.

What’s inside

Each part answers one question you have.

Market entry

Solves“Is this niche winnable at my price?”

Viability, competitor sizing, barriers to entry and a clear go / no-go before money moves.

Research

Solves“Which keywords actually carry demand?”

Keyword map, demand curve, seasonality — the search data the listing and the ads are built on.

Listing

Solves“Why would a stranger click, then buy?”

Copy, images, A+ and backend terms written from the shopper’s side of the screen.

PPC

Solves“Where does the first dollar of ad spend go?”

Launch campaign structure, budget pacing and ranking targets for the first 90 days.

Launch strategy

Solves“What does traction look like, and by when?”

The 90-day plan: review velocity, ranking milestones and what triggers a change of course.

Is this your path?

Who it’s for, and who it isn’t.

This is for you if

  • A first product, or a new product line
  • An established brand launching a new ASIN
  • Inventory and ad budget already set aside
  • Willing to hold to a 90-day plan

It probably isn’t, if

  • You want to test ten products for a few hundred dollars.
  • The product is already live and stalled. See Growth →
  • The account is suspended or a listing is down. See Recovery →
  • You want someone to “just run ads”.

Questions sellers ask first

Before you ask.

Do you take over my account?

No. We work inside your Seller Central with user permissions. You own the account, the listings and the data.

What if Diagnose says “don’t launch”?

Then you don’t. You keep the report and the inventory money. It happens, and it’s the cheapest bad launch you’ll ever have.

Do you guarantee page one?

Nobody can honestly guarantee rank. We commit to the plan and the scorecard, and we show you the numbers every week.

Who actually does the work?

A named lead who runs your launch, plus specialists for listing and PPC. Not a rotating pool.

Can I stop after Build and launch it myself?

Yes. The playbook is yours. Most clients keep us for Activate because the first 90 days are where launches are won or lost.
