The Amazon Launch Framework™

Six stages from opportunity to traction.

Each stage answers a different launch question. Together they create the foundation, traffic system and feedback loop required to make early performance measurable.

01 · MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Know whether the opportunity is winnable.

Understand demand, competition, gaps, pricing and the customer problem before committing heavily to inventory or advertising.

  • Market opportunity map
  • Competitor analysis
  • Keyword universe
  • Category opportunity
  • Demand assessment
02 · PRODUCT & POSITIONING

Give the product a reason to win.

Define why someone should choose this product and turn that answer into a clear commercial position.

  • Positioning
  • Value proposition
  • Differentiation
  • Pricing strategy
  • Competitive angle
03 · LISTING ARCHITECTURE

Build the conversion foundation.

Structure the listing around how shoppers move from search to confidence and purchase.

  • Title & bullets
  • Description
  • Backend keywords
  • Image strategy
  • A+ & Brand Store
04 · LAUNCH ADVERTISING

Turn paid traffic into intelligence.

Launch advertising with a deliberate progression from discovery to validated search terms and scalable winners.

  • Discovery
  • Keyword harvesting
  • Search-term validation
  • Conversion winners
  • Exact-match scaling
05 · CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

Make every qualified visit work harder.

Monitor the signals that tell you whether traffic is turning into commercial momentum.

  • CTR
  • CVR
  • Sessions
  • Add-to-cart behavior
  • Image & offer performance
06 · TRACTION & SCALE

Know when the launch is actually working.

The launch isn't finished when the ASIN goes live. It is finished when the business has repeatable signals worth scaling.

  • Repeatable sales
  • Converting keywords
  • Emerging organic rankings
  • Controlled advertising
  • Predictable economics

The first 90 days

Launch with milestones—not hope.

The exact cadence changes by category and product, but the operating principle stays the same: establish the foundation, learn from the market, then scale validated signals.

DAYS 01–30

Foundation + discovery

Validate the market, finalize positioning, build the listing, activate initial advertising and establish the baseline scorecard.

  • Market & competitor intelligence
  • Listing + creative foundation
  • Initial PPC activation
  • Baseline metrics
DAYS 31–60

Validation + optimization

Separate useful demand from noise and improve the listing and advertising system around actual shopper behavior.

  • Search-term validation
  • Conversion optimization
  • Creative refinement
  • Budget reallocation
DAYS 61–90

Traction + controlled scale

Concentrate investment around signals that are proving repeatable and establish the next growth milestones.

  • Winning keyword expansion
  • Organic ranking support
  • Repeatable sales signals
  • Next-stage roadmap
