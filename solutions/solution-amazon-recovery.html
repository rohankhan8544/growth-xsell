The Methodology

Five steps.
One recovery loop.

The problem tells us where to start. The methodology tells us what to do next—without treating every Amazon issue the same way.

01 · DIAGNOSE

Find out what changed.

Check the product, account, and recent performance before making another change.

What changed · Where · Why
02 · PRIORITIZE

Fix what matters first.

Separate the issue that is blocking sales from problems that can wait.

Impact · Risk · Urgency
03 · STABILIZE

Stop the problem getting worse.

Clear the immediate blocker so the account can operate normally again.

Health · Catalog · Compliance
04 · RESTORE

Bring performance back.

Repair the part of the sales engine that was affected.

SEO · Creative · PPC
05 · PROTECT

Keep the problem from returning.

Put simple checks around the issue so the recovery lasts.

Monitoring · Reporting · Controls
