The situation

The brand was generating demand, but its advertising account was paying too much to capture it. Broad targeting, duplicated search terms, and weak budget rules made performance look busy without making it more efficient.

Our first move was not to spend more. It was to separate profitable demand from expensive noise and give each campaign a job it could be measured against.

What changed

ROAS2.9X
ACoS34%
Sales$27K

The work behind the result

01 / FIND THE LEAK

Rebuilt the account view

We grouped campaigns by product economics, intent, and role so spend could be judged against contribution rather than clicks alone.

02 / PROTECT EFFICIENCY

Moved budget toward proof

Winning search terms received cleaner budgets while weak placements and overlapping targets were reduced or removed.

03 / REINVEST WITH INTENT

Turned savings into growth

Once efficiency stabilized, the recovered budget supported the products and audiences with the clearest path to incremental sales.
